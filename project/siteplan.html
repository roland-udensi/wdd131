1. Site Name

✥ The Igbo Heritage

The name "The Igbo Heritage" represents the cultural identity, traditions, history, and values of the Igbo people of southeastern Nigeria. I chose this name because I want to help people understand and appreciate Igbo culture and preserve knowledge of its traditions for future generations.

Proposed tagline: Celebrating Culture, Preserving Traditions.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of The Igbo Heritage is to educate visitors about the culture and traditions of the Igbo people of southeastern Nigeria. The website will provide information about traditional clothing, festivals, food, marriage traditions, language, and cultural values. Through informative articles, images, and interactive features, visitors will be able to explore Igbo heritage, understand its cultural significance, and develop a greater appreciation for Igbo traditions.

3. Scenarios

Visitors might ask the following questions:

  • What traditional clothes do Igbo men and women wear during cultural ceremonies?
  • What are some important Igbo festivals, and why are they celebrated?
  • What traditional foods are popular among the Igbo people?
  • What happens during a traditional Igbo marriage ceremony?
